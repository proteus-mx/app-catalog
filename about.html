Our story & mission

Do one thing, and do it beautifully

Sapphire Studios was founded by Paul Thornton to make the kind of apps we love to use: quick to pick up, satisfying to play and polished down to the last detail. Rather than one sprawling product, we build a growing family of small apps, each focused on doing one thing well.

Our mission is simple: make small apps that feel brilliant. Games you can enjoy in a spare minute, puzzles that keep your mind sharp, and learning tools and utilities that make everyday things a little easier.

Today that adds up to 33 apps on the App Store, every one of them free to download, and more are on the way.

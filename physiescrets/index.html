It’s a Kid Thing

Physie Secrets

by Kristen Thornton

Strength Fun Confidence

A funny, diary-style story

Sophie has absolutely no idea what Physie is.

One minute she’s a normal eleven-year-old. The next, she’s trying to remember a bunch of strange moves, make new friends and avoid making a complete fool of herself.

And it’s not going particularly well.

There’s a LOT to remember, her feet don’t always cooperate, and now there are competitions. In front of actual judges!

What could possibly go wrong?

Well… pretty much everything.

But somewhere along the way, Sophie discovers that Physie might actually be fun.

But why doesn’t Kayla like her?

A funny, illustrated diary about friendship, embarrassing moments, competition nerves and finding the courage to give things a go.

Friends make everything brighter

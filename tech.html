The pipeline

Step by step, idea to release

What happens at each step, and the tools that do the work.

  1. 01

    Step 1: Spark an idea

    Ideas come from play, player feedback and our community contributors. We explore them in conversation with ChatGPT and Grok, and capture and tidy notes with Apple Intelligence.

    • ChatGPT chat mode
    • Grok
    • Apple Intelligence

    HITLIdea approval Paul and Sophie decide which ideas go further.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Engineer the prompt

    The idea becomes a clear brief: what we’re making, who it’s for, the constraints and how we’ll know it works. Good prompts are reused and refined across various GPT and Grok models.

    • Prompt engineering
    • Various GPT and Grok models
  3. 03

    Step 3: Plan & build with agents

    ChatGPT work mode and our Grok Bot agents take the brief and work through it step by step: Onyx on features and fixes, Opal on icons and screens, Topaz on copy.

    • ChatGPT work mode
    • Grok Bot
    OnyxEngineering LeadOpalDesign LeadTopazMarketing Lead
  4. 04

    Step 4: Code, build & version

    Code is edited in VS Code and Xcode. Xcode compiles and signs each build, and every change is committed to GitHub so it can be reviewed and rolled back.

    • VS Code
    • Xcode
    • GitHub

    HITLCode & build review A human reviews the code and plays the build before testing.

  5. 05

    Step 5: Simulate

    Builds run first in iOS device simulators across different iPhone and iPad screen sizes, catching layout and logic issues early.

    • iOS device simulators
    • Xcode
    JasperQuality Assurance Lead
  6. 06

    Step 6: Alpha on our own devices

    Alpha builds go onto our own test devices to check real-world feel, performance and hardware features.

    • Alpha & beta test devices
    JasperQuality Assurance Lead
  7. 07

    Step 7: Beta with TestFlight

    Xcode uploads the build to App Store Connect and TestFlight delivers it to beta testers’ devices, including our community contributors Kristen, Alice and Mary. Their feedback shapes the final polish.

    • TestFlight
    • App Store Connect
    • Alpha & beta test devices
  8. 08

    Step 8: Ship with App Store Connect

    The store listing, screenshots and creative are set up in App Store Connect, the build is submitted for App Review, and once approved it’s released on the App Store.

    • App Store Connect
    TopazMarketing LeadOpalDesign Lead

    HITLRelease go/no-go Only a human can press go on a release.

  9. 09

    Step 9: Learn & loop back

    App Store Connect analytics, player reviews and tester feedback show what’s working, and feed straight into the next idea.

    • App Store Connect
    • TestFlight

The toolchain

What each tool does

14 tools across four layers, each with one clear job.

01 Ideate

  • Prompt engineering

    Turning a rough idea into a clear, reusable brief, with goals, audience, constraints and checks, so AI tools give focused, consistent results.

  • ChatGPT chat mode

    Conversational brainstorming, research and problem-solving, from first concepts to tricky bugs.

  • Grok

    A second conversational partner for ideas, research and fresh perspectives on a problem.

  • Apple Intelligence

    Apple’s built-in tools on our own devices for capturing, summarising and polishing notes and text.

02 Build

  • ChatGPT work mode

    Agent mode that takes a brief and works through multi-step tasks, then reports back for review.

  • Grok Bot

    Home of our AI agents, Onyx, Topaz, Opal and Jasper, who carry out engineering, marketing, design and QA tasks.

  • Various GPT and Grok models

    Different models for different jobs, chosen per task, from quick drafts to deeper reasoning.

  • VS Code

    A lightweight editor for code, scripts, web pages and config.

  • Xcode

    Apple’s developer tool: builds, signs and archives our apps, runs the simulators and uploads builds.

  • GitHub

    Source control for every change: versioned, reviewed and backed up. It also hosts this website.

03 Test

  • iOS device simulators

    Run each build in Xcode on virtual iPhones and iPads of different sizes before it touches a real device.

  • Alpha & beta test devices

    Real hardware for testing: alpha builds on our own devices first, then beta builds on testers’ devices.

  • TestFlight

    Apple’s beta service: installs pre-release builds on testers’ devices and collects their feedback.

04 Ship

  • App Store Connect

    Where each app is managed: builds, store listing, screenshots, pricing, App Review, release and analytics.

The toolchain

What each tool does

24 tools, languages and frameworks, each with one clear job.

01 Ideate

  • Prompt engineering

    Turning a rough idea into a clear, reusable brief, with goals, audience, constraints and checks, so AI tools give focused, consistent results.

  • ChatGPT chat mode

    Conversational brainstorming, research and problem-solving, from first concepts to tricky bugs.

  • Grok

    A second conversational partner for ideas, research and fresh perspectives on a problem.

  • Apple Intelligence

    Apple’s built-in tools on our own devices for capturing, summarising and polishing notes and text.

02 Build

  • ChatGPT work mode

    Agent mode that takes a brief and works through multi-step tasks, then reports back for review.

  • Grok Bot

    Home of our AI agents, Onyx, Topaz, Opal and Jasper, who carry out engineering, marketing, design and QA tasks.

  • Various GPT and Grok models

    Different models for different jobs, chosen per task, from quick drafts to deeper reasoning.

  • VS Code

    A lightweight editor for code, scripts, web pages and config.

  • Xcode

    Apple’s developer tool: builds, signs and archives our apps, runs the simulators and uploads builds.

  • GitHub

    Source control for every change: versioned, reviewed and backed up. It also hosts this website.

03 Test

  • iOS device simulators

    Run each build in Xcode on virtual iPhones and iPads of different sizes before it touches a real device.

  • Alpha & beta test devices

    Real hardware for testing: alpha builds on our own devices first, then beta builds on testers’ devices.

  • TestFlight

    Apple’s beta service: installs pre-release builds on testers’ devices and collects their feedback.

04 Ship

  • App Store Connect

    Where each app is managed: builds, store listing, screenshots, pricing, App Review, release and analytics.

  • Feature toggles

    Switches that let a new feature ship turned off in a live app, be piloted with a small group, then be turned on gradually or rolled back without a new release.

+ Languages, frameworks & services

  • Swift

    Apple’s programming language, used for our apps on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

  • JavaScript

    Brings our web experiences, and this website, to life with interactivity.

  • HTML

    The structure and content of our web pages, including this site.

  • CSS

    The look, layout and motion of our web pages, from colour to responsive design.

  • Python

    Tooling and automation, such as this site’s generator and our App Store Connect scripts.

  • three.js

    A JavaScript library for real-time 3D graphics in the browser.

  • CloudKit

    Apple’s framework for storing app data in iCloud and syncing it across a player’s devices.

  • StoreKit

    Apple’s framework for in-app purchases and subscriptions. Apple processes every payment.

  • Google AdMob

    Google’s mobile ads platform, used to show ads in our free apps, with reports on how they perform.

The journey

Discover, design, develop, deliver

Four stages, each led by one of our AI agents and steered by Paul and Sophie. Look for the HITL checkpoints: the moments a human decides what happens next.

  1. 01 Discover

    Find the spark

    AI agents lead

    Marketing LeadDesign Lead

    Scan App Store trends, reviews and player feedback, and turn promising ideas into quick concept sketches to react to.

    Humans

    Sophie and Paul: bring the experience and the instinct: which ideas are fun, on-brand and worth building.

    HITLIdea approval Nothing moves into design until a human says yes.

  2. 02 Design

    Shape it beautifully

    AI agents lead

    Design Lead

    Explore app icons, App Store creative, splash screens and UI polish in lots of variations, quickly.

    Humans

    Sophie and Paul: choose the direction and refine the feel, so every app looks and feels like Sapphire.

    HITLDesign sign-off A human signs off the look before any code is written.

  3. 03 Develop

    Build it brilliantly

    AI agents lead

    Engineering Lead

    Build features, fix bugs and prepare builds for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

    Humans

    Paul: set the technical direction, review the code and play every build.

    HITLCode & build review Code and builds are reviewed by a human before they go to testing.

  4. 04 Deliver

    Ship it with care

    AI agents lead

    Quality Assurance LeadMarketing Lead

    Test builds across devices and check quality, then draft the App Store copy and social content for launch.

    Humans

    Paul and Sophie: make the final call on quality, the store listing and launch.

    HITLRelease go/no-go Only a human can press go on a release.

Learn and loop back

After launch, App Store Connect and AdMob insights, together with player reviews and feedback, show us what people love. That feeds straight back into the next Discover, so the cycle keeps turning.

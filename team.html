The people behind the apps

Meet the team.

Sapphire Studios is a two-person studio powered by a team of AI agents, behind 33 apps and games for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

Leadership

The people in charge

Paul Thornton

Founder & Chief Technology Officer

Paul founded Sapphire Studios and designs, builds and ships every one of its apps and games, from the first idea to the App Store release.

He leads the studio’s technology and product direction, and its AI-assisted workflows, which help a small studio design, test and launch more polished apps.

FounderTechnologyProductAI workflows

Sophie Thornton

Junior Brand, Digital Marketing & AI Coordinator

Sophie looks after the Sapphire Studios brand and online presence: creating social media content and helping more people discover our apps.

She coordinates the studio’s team of AI agents on creative, marketing and business tasks, works on App Store listings, tests new games and suggests features. She also tracks downloads, engagement and advertising performance with App Store Connect and Google AdMob, and leads small creative projects of her own.
